Project Overview

The purpose of the website is to provide industry leading services and electrical products that meet the current requirements while keeping it future proof for both your home and business, all of this while complying with the standards and safety requirements.

Website goals and Objectives

Looking at the current market, we are looking to create a clear message to our consumers of the services we offer.
The website will assist with providing visibility and advertising the services and products we offer.
To provide knowledge to customers a base line of what is required for their individual needs before we even go out and assist with installations or repairs.
Success will be measured by both the traffic and the enquires/sales received from the site to see what the clients are mainly in the market for. This will provide a clear indication of the type of products we need to have in stock.

Key Features and Functionality

The website would have the company logo on the top left part of the homepage with About us, Services, Products and Contact us on the furthest Right of the page
Below that we will provide the client with our emergency call out options which will consist of out WhatsApp & cell phone number.
Below that it will be information which will provide a detailed offering of products and services.
Below that we will show our most asked questions

Timeline and Milestone

The project will take 3-4 month to complete. The first milestone which involves planning and the creation of a basic site would be done by August. The Second part which involves the adding of the design and visuals should be done by September, with the final product inclusive of functionality and SOE should be in November.
