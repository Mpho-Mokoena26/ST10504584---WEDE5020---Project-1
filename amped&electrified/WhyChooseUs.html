Projects/Why Choose Us

Quality Electrical Work. Reliable Results.

At Amped & Electrified, we understand that every electrical project is different. Our approach is to understand the customer's requirements before recommending an appropriate solution. From smaller residential jobs to larger commercial projects, we focus on delivering work that is safe, practical and professionally completed.

Our Approach

1. Understand We listen to your requirements and assess the electrical work needed.

2. Plan We determine the appropriate solution and explain the work that needs to be completed.

3. Install or Repair Our experienced electricians carry out the required work with attention to safety and quality.

4. Test & Check We check the completed work to ensure that the electrical installation or repair is functioning correctly.

5. Complete We leave the work area organised and ensure that the customer understands the completed work.
