About Amped & Electrified

Amped & Electrified (A&E) was established on 1 January 2026 by two experienced electricians who shared a vision of making professional electrical services easier to access for homeowners and businesses. The business was created to provide customers with a dependable electrical service that combines experience, quality workmanship and affordability. We understand that electrical problems can disrupt your home, business and daily activities. That is why we focus on providing practical solutions while maintaining high standards of safety and professionalism.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, reliable and affordable electrical services that meet the needs of our residential and commercial customers. We aim to build long-term relationships with our customers through honest communication, quality workmanship and dependable service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted electrical services provider known for quality, reliability and customer satisfaction
